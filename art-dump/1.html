Józef Chełmoński Art Dump

While looking at pieces for another post I kept looking at art from Józef Chełmoński. While I didn't use any of them I loved them so much I'm putting them here.

Józef Chełmoński

[credit line here]

This piece-wow. This was the first one I saw by him and it made me want to rewrite the crucifixion to be more cold and emphasize Christ's loneliness. I'm glad I didn't though because ultimtely I don't think this piece is about Christ, it's about the faithful in Eastern Europe. When I see this painting it reminds me of a simple but loving faith.
Chełmoński's Wikipedia calls him a "realist" painter; that's what stands out to me, this painting is so real.

[credit line here]

I'm going to start with what Chełmoński's seems to have painted most: people with horses. Given there are so many of these I'm not showing I wonder if these were very lucrative to make. I bet rich people loved buying paintings of dogs hunting and horses frothing.
