Rubáiyát of Omar Khayyám, the Astronomer-Poet of Persia

While looking at works by Elihu Vedder I found that his most famous work is a collection of drawings for an edition of this book.
To allow readers to experience these drawings in their original contet I've used high quality scans from the Smithsonian with an optional overlay that makes the text of the poetry more readable.
Note: in the orignal Persian these poems were not compiled into a single volume. When Edward FitzGerald compiled and translated these he took poems from many places the poet wrote.
