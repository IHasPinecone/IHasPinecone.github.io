Ok, but what is that page?

If you didn't realize it already, everything on that page is procedurally generated. It's basically a list of random verbs and nouns put together the old fashion way; absolutely nothing on that page was written via a large language model like ChatGPT. Well, the code partially was, but more on that later.

I made this page because I love stories told through randomness and a well crafted table. You see this sort of thing a lot in Old School tabletop RPGs. You encounter an old man NPC, who might be super generic, except the GM rolls on the table and see the old man is a... former poet who... collects birds. Suddenly that randomly rolled NPC has a lot of character. What do those two things tell you about him? What might his personality be like? So yeah that's the sort of thing I wanted to make with this page. I wanted to paint really interesting vignettes with things randomly pulled off the table. I hope this is successful and it starts coughing up cute little stories.

I think at one point I wanted things on the table to be as mundane as possible, just make the whole thing really boring. But not gonna lie, I made too much fun writing some of these entries. In fact many individual entires I think serve as a sort of mirror version of my brother's beloved brainwaves page where random things on my mind are phrased in a funny way and recorded forever.

When writing entries I kept everything in the realm of possibility or things I've already done. (Yes I've met the wife of the lead singer of Blue's Traveller, she lives near my hometown.) I think it makes the whole thing much funnier if I'm lying about really mundane things I've done. One of my friends also pointed out this kinda works as a commentary on para-social relationships. Like, I'm giving freakingly frequent updates on mundane things in my life, and yeah keeping everything in the realm of possibility I think also emphasizes that side of it. Yeah I think it is kinda commentary on that sure.

This is certainly the most ambitious thing I've coded for this website. Not gonna lie it's embarrasing how much time I put into making this thing work. I've basically taught myself everything I know about coding, and certainly everything I know about Javasript and HTML. Of course, "self-taught" has a whole different meaning in the world of these AI bots who can write really mediocre code. If you visit my Github Page you can parouse all the code I wrote for this entire website. About half of it is written by ChatGPT. I'm not proud of it, but this updates page specifically gave me a lot of headaches so I gave up about halfway through.
