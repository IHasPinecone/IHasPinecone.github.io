Songs from Space Log

Record of songs I've caught from among the stars

I have a relay set up on my roof to catch radio frequencies from space. Radio frequencies tend to bounce around space for a while and sometimes they are reflected in such a way that they are directed right back at earth. Through this I get exposed to music in a weird order.

Every Sunday I sit down and listen to what frequencies I happened to pick up returning from space this week. Starting the first week of advent, return every Sunday to learn what radio frequencies I heard back.
