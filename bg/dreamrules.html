Dreamrules

Dreamrules is a full rules-overhaul for the Dune board game. The game was originally released by Avalon Hill in 1979 and has been reprinted with rules changes by Gale Force Nine in 2019 and 2026. Dreamrules began by fixing gameplay/balance issues with the base game, and has since expanded to completely reworking existing factions and creating new ones.
