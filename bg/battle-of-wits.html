Battle of Wits Rules

"The wizard who reads 34,000 cards is powerful. The wizard who memorizes 34,000 cards is insane."

1. Starter Card: Random surprise starter or custom typed starting card.

2. 40 Starting Life: Each player starts with 40 life points.

3. Vintage Legal Cardpool (FNM Promos Excluded): Cards played must be legal in Vintage. Promo printings are allowed except for Friday Night Magic (FNM) promos.

4. Strict Connection Criteria: Must share at least 2 connections. Subtypes and Supertypes/Types require exact matches.

5. Mana Value Connection: Having the same mana value (e.g., 1GG and 2W both have 3 MV) counts as 1 connection.

6. Flavor Text Source Connection: Cards can connect if their flavor text attribution (after the – dash) is identical.

7. Set Multipliers (Non-additive): Exact set match gives x2 multiplier. Same set group match gives x1.5 multiplier (all core sets link to each other, all archenemy sets link to each other, all planechase sets link to each other, and blocks/associated products group together via metadata). Set connections do not contribute (+1) to the connection count.

8. 45-Second Timer: You have 45 seconds per turn.
