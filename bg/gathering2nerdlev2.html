Duel Variant Rules

1. Objective: Both players start at 20 Life. Deal damage to your opponent by connecting cards. First to 0 life loses.

2. Connection Rules: Each individually matching Type and Subtype counts as a separate connection! (e.g. Artifact Creature counts as 2 type connections).

3. Minimum Connections: Submitted cards must share at least 2 connections with the previous card.

4. Damage & Expansion Bonus: Each valid connection deals 1 damage. If the cards share a Shared Expansion / Set, damage is doubled!

5. Live Preview: As you type or select a card, the game instantly previews incoming damage and matching connections so you can refine your play.
