Summon the Stars: A Coop Variant for Wingspan

(yeah you read that right)

Summon the Stars is an experimental board game Rorschach test. Players work together to win the game, but which win condition the group pursues reveals conscious/unconscious biases of the players.

Players will play a mostly standard game of Wingspan, based on how many points they score as a group and whether they balance the humors (more on that later) will determine which ending the table acheives. Endings both have immediate rewards (such satisfaction for beating a difficult challenge and signing the board) and legacy rewards (new content/character upgrades).

Setup

To play you will need a standard copy of Wingspan, and a printout of the humors board, and a character sheet for each player.
(Click here for a pdf of the humors board) (Click here for a pdf of the character sheets) (X pieces to track the clock. Eggs can be used.)

Setup a standard game for 2-5 players.
Do not deal out starting bonus cards (although players may gain bonus cards through other means)
Do not setup the objectives board. (Players may still use the objective board to count how many rounds have passed.)
Players shall choose a difficulty level. Consult the following chart for suggested difficulty based on number of players. Remember upgraded characters are more powerful so they can play at higher difficulties. Place the clock pawns according to difficulty.
If this is the first game, players shall choose one of the starting character sheets and sign it to claim it as theirs. Players will take starting birds indicated on their character sheet, plus an additional 3 random birds from the deck.

Play

Players will take their turns simultaneously, in any order they wish. Players may wish to sequence their turns to fully utilize birds that give resources to all players.
Each player begins the round with 7 action cubes. After a player has used a cube they shall leave it in the habitat as in a standard game. After all players have used all their action cubes, proceed to the humors step.

(cubes in habitats are used to balance/imbalance humors. explanation goes here)

Turning the Clock

Whenever a point is scored (bird played, foot cached, card tucked) turn the clock back that many points. Birds only move the clock according to their feather number, birds are not natively worth 1 point (note this is different from the base game).
Eggs and bonus cards do not immediatly move the clock. Eggs/bonus cards are counted and move the clock only at the end of round 4.
All players then collect their 7 cubes, rotate the clock forward, and begin the next round. Play continues until 4 rounds have passed, after which players determine the end game condition based.
Before balancing the humors, if the invader piece is greater or equal to the player piece the game ends immediatly in an anarchy victory (see below). If the game has not ended move the invader piece up one level and continue the game.

Win Conditions

An ending is determined based on the following chart. Endings with balanced humors generally benefit everyone while unbalanced humors affect only the player with the most eggs.

Anarchy (the clock reaches maximum count)

All birds on player boards are sent to the void. They are not shuffled into the deck in future games.
(starting birds listed on player sheets are not sent to the void)

Circle (unbalanced humors, clock is not 0)

The player with the most eggs fills in a star on their character sheet for every player in the game. They are encouraged to thank the other players.
(Ex. in a 3 player game the player will fill in 3 stars)

Cooperate (balanced humors, clock is not 0)

Every player fills in a single star on their character sheet. Give yourselves a pat on the back.

Ascend (unbalanced humors, clock is 0)

The player with the most eggs fills in all the stars of their character's constellation and retires their character. That player signs their name and their characters name under one of the constellations, and can now play as the character unlocked with that constellation. Unlockable characters have entirely custom bird cards and unique powers. Go to (url) to view new character sheet.

Conquor (balanced humors, clock is 0)

All players fills in two stars on their character sheet. All players also write their initials on that difficulty level showing they accomplished it.

Tied Egg Count

If the highets number of eggs is tied, the following are tie breakers (in order): Most unused food, total number of birds, most cached food, most tucked cards. If all of those are tied, the oldest player wins ties as a reward for their service to the stars.

Expansions

Players are free to add extra bird packs, but proper content expansions run the risk of making the game too easy. Players more familiar with the expansions are free to tweak numbers to levels that seem appropriate. Conquoring numbers are supposed to be difficult to achieve on a first play, but possible after a few upgrades. Remember players start the game with 6 birds instead of 3 and have a unique player power.
