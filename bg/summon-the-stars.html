Summon the Stars: A Coop Variant for Wingspan

(yeah you read that right)

We all value games differently. Some of us love winning above all else, but most have other more subtle and unconscious biases. Summon the Stars forces players to expose what they value most in the game, whether consciously or unconscoiusly through play. Players will work together to score points to turn back the game's clock, and may optionally work together to balance the humors. After the final round, players will achieve a unique ending based on how far the clock has been turned back and whether the humors are balanced. Different endings both have inherint rewards (such as signing the board and satisfaction for beating a difficult challenge) and legacy rewards (new content and character upgrades)

This game is a highly experimental proof of concept. If you played the game or if the rulebook got you thinking I'd love to hear it. You can email me at [EMAIL].

Play

Players will take their turns entirely simultaneously, in any order they wish. Players may wish to sequence their turns to fully utilize birds that give resources such as food at all players.
Each player begins the round with 7 action cubes. After a player has used a cube they shall leave it in the habitat as in a standard game. All cubes shall be removed during the balancing humors step.

After all players have taken their turn players may use the cubes in their habitats to balance the humors until all players choose not to balance with any more cube. All players then collect their 7 cubes, rotate the clock forward, and begin the next round. Play continues until 4 rounds have passed, after which players determine the end game condition based.

Win Conditions

An ending is selected based on the following chart. Endings with balanced humors generally benefit everyone while unbalanced humors generally affect only the player with the most eggs.

Anarchy (the clock reaches maximum count)

All birds on player boards are sent to the void. They are not shuffled into the deck in future games.
(starting birds listed on player sheets are not sent to the void)

Circle (unbalanced humors, clock is not 0)

The player with the most eggs fills in a star on their character sheet for every player in the game. They are encouraged to thank the other players.
(Ex. in a 3 player game the player will fill in 3 stars)

Cooperate (balanced humors, clock is not 0)

Every player fills in a single star on their character sheet. Give yourselves a pat on the back.

Ascend (unbalanced humors, clock is 0)

The player with the most eggs fills in the rest of the stars on their character constellation and glues it onto the margins of the board. That player now goes to (URL) to unlock a new character. New characters have custom birds cards and more unique powers.

Conquor (balanced humors, clock is 0)

All players level up their character is during a cooperation victory, but all players also write their initials around the clock at the difficulty they played at. Players shall go to (URL) to unlock their new difficulty.

Tied Egg Count

If the highets number of eggs is tied, the following are tie breakers (in order): Most unused food, total number of birds, most cached food, most tucked cards. If all of those are tied, the oldest player wins ties as a reward for their service to the stars.

Expansions

Players are free to add extra bird packs, but proper content expansions run the risk of making the game too easy. Players more familiar with the expansions are free to tweak numbers to levels that seem appropriate. Conquoring numbers are supposed to be difficult to achieve on a first play, but possible after a few upgrades. Remember players start the game with 6 birds instead of 3 and have a unique player power.
